# 0.13 Decision: docs-independent differential corpus comparing stdout

*Broken out of "0.8 Decision: code-review findings triage"
(lot:033lMgJ9BtS8IZM2KpoBXi), finding F3, at your request. Re-verified on main
2026-07-28.*

## Problem

`tests/differential.rs` — the harness that validates the wasm backend against
the interpreter (the semantics oracle) — iterates only `docs/examples.json`
and compares only the final `to-string` value. Two blind spots follow:

1. **Effect streams are invisible.** Printed output, effect ordering, and
   duplication are never compared — only final values. (This is exactly why
   the F1 double-print fallback bug could not be caught by any test.)
2. **Anything not exercised by a documented example is untested**: arithmetic
   edge cases, out-of-bounds, deep recursion, cell mutation ordering, and
   multi-call resource-state persistence.

## The question

Do you accept this as work to schedule?

- A docs-independent `tests/fixtures/` differential corpus exercising the
  resource/cell/persist surface, including multi-call sequences.
- Comparing the **stdout stream** as well as the final value.
- Optionally, a small property/fuzz layer over arithmetic/list/string ops.
- Keep the SKIP ratchet.

## Recommendation

Accept, high priority — the original triage put F3 first or alongside F1/F2,
because it is what makes their regressions visible. F1 has since been resolved
as "drop the fallback" (0.8), which removes one bug class F3 would have
caught, but stdout comparison still guards the backend's own effect behaviour
and the fixtures corpus covers F2's persist surface.

## How to answer

One update: "accept" / "reject", plus whether to include the property/fuzz
layer.
