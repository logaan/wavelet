Implementation gaps under design review: there is no general local loop/recur or while form, and local Let functions cannot recursively reference their own binding. A runtime-length fold therefore needs a general Wasm control-flow primitive, not intrinsic map/fold emitter logic. Loop/Recur with loop-carried values is preferred for predictable allocation; While plus cells is a simpler but more allocation-heavy alternative. Generated Let/Fn binding keys also cannot currently be unquoted; a general compile-time record-form constructor or improved quasiquote bindings may be needed for hygienic generated loop bindings. Parent is coordinating the control-flow decision before dependent implementation.
